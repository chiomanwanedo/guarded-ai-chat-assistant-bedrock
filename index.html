Welcome to Sparrow Lane Cycles

We're a small, independent bike shop tucked away on Sparrow Lane in Leeds. Whether your tyre's gone flat on the way to work, your gears need a proper tune-up, or you fancy exploring the city on two wheels, we're here to help – no fuss, no jargon.

Every repair comes with our 30-day workmanship guarantee, and we offer free collection and delivery within 3 miles on full services. Just give us a call or drop in.
